Streak — support

We answer like
it matters. It does.

If gambling is harming you right now

Streak is a support tool, not a crisis service. If you're in immediate distress, please contact your local gambling helpline or emergency services. In Australia, Gambling Help Online offers free, confidential support 24/7 at 1800 858 858. Most countries operate an equivalent free helpline — search "gambling help" plus your country.

In-app support

The fastest route for account, streak or billing questions is Settings → Help inside the app. Messages sent there reach a human on our team, typically within a few hours, day or night.

Email

Prefer email? Write to support@streak.app. Include the email on your account and, if relevant, a screenshot — it usually saves a round trip.

Send us feedback

Have a suggestion, found a bug, or just want to tell us something? Leave it here — a real person reads every message.

Common questions

I relapsed. Do I lose everything?
No. Your streak counter resets, but your history, insights and milestones stay. A reset is a data point, not a verdict — the app is built around coming back.

Can I change my accountability partner?
Yes, any time, in Settings → Accountability. Your partner is only ever notified of what you've chosen to share.

How does pricing work?
Streak Pro is $15 a month, or $80 a year (about 56% less). Every feature is included — unlimited check-ins, Spyke, the community, the Learn library, all of it. Subscriptions renew automatically and you can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Is Spyke a real person?
Spyke is our AI support companion — available every hour of every day. He's a tool for the hard minutes, not a replacement for professional help.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their standard policies. If you hit a wall, email us and we'll help you through the process.
